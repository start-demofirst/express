## ⚡ Productivity Tips

### Time Management
1. **Use Pomodoro Technique** - 25 min focus, 5 min break
2. **Batch Similar Tasks** - Group theory, practice, and review
3. **Use Commute Time** - Listen to coding podcasts
4. **Lunch Break Learning** - 15 min reading during lunch

### Energy Management
1. **Morning Peak** - Complex topics when energy is high
2. **Evening Practice** - Hands-on coding when focused
3. **Night Review** - Light learning to wind down
4. **Weekend Recovery** - Deep work when rested

### Learning Efficiency
1. **Active Learning** - Code while learning, don't just watch
2. **Spaced Repetition** - Review concepts regularly
3. **Project-Based** - Apply knowledge to real projects
4. **Documentation** - Keep learning notes organized

## 🚨 Flexibility Guidelines

### When You're Tired
- **Reduce session time** by 15-30 minutes
- **Focus on review** rather than new concepts
- **Use passive learning** (videos, reading)
- **Skip one session** if needed, but never two in a row

### When You're Motivated
- **Extend sessions** by 15-30 minutes
- **Tackle complex topics**
- **Start new projects**
- **Learn advanced concepts**

### When Work is Busy
- **Maintain minimum** 30 min daily learning
- **Focus on weekends** for major progress
- **Use mobile learning** during commute
- **Keep momentum** with small wins
